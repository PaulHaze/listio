# Midnight Movies — Top-Level Groupings

Proposed top-level groups for the Nuvio collections page. Catalogs and titles stay as-is in `midnight_movies.md`; these groups only organise catalogs inside Nuvio.

Alternative names are listed after each group heading. "(alt)" marks a catalog that could live in more than one group.

- Something Wrong in Suburbia: the white-picket-fence-hiding-rot side, like Blue Velvet.
- Neon Nightmares: urban and dreamy, and fits next to Neon Noir.
- Dreams That Bleed: lurid with a hint of violence.
- The Other Side of Midnight Street: urban plus fever dream.
